Using AI Agents as a Learning Partner

AI agents are becoming part of my learning workflow. I want them to help me find gaps, generate practice tasks, review code, and turn vague questions into concrete next steps.

Useful roles

  • Reading assistant: summarize a paper, extract definitions, and list claims to verify.
  • Coding partner: review a notebook, find fragile assumptions, and suggest tests.
  • Study coach: create spaced review questions from my own notes.
  • Research log assistant: convert experiment results into a clear write-up.

The danger

The biggest risk is fluency without understanding. A polished explanation still needs to be re-derived, tested, and written in my own words. Smooth output is not knowledge.

My working principle

I will use agents to accelerate feedback, not to skip difficulty. The hard parts are the learning: debugging, proving, testing, and explaining.
