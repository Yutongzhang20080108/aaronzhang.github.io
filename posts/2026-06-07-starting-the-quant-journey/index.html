Starting My Quant Finance Journey

This is the first entry in my public learning journal. Quant finance rewards curiosity, but it also demands structure, repetition, experiments, and clear explanations.

Why quant finance?

Quant finance combines math, markets, programming, and decisions under uncertainty. I want to understand how a statistical pattern becomes a tested strategy, and how a strategy becomes a robust research process.

What I need to build

  • A stronger foundation in probability, statistics, and linear algebra.
  • Better Python habits for data analysis, testing, and reproducible research.
  • Market intuition: assets, liquidity, risk, transaction costs, and incentives.
  • A habit of writing down assumptions before running experiments.

My first rule

Every project should produce an artifact: a note, chart, backtest, notebook, or short post. If I cannot explain what I learned, I have not learned it carefully enough yet.
